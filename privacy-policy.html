Effective 2026-09-13

This tool connects only to accounts that belong to the operator. It does not collect, process, sell or share personal data of any other person. It has no visitors, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers.

What it accesses

YouTube
Reads the operator's channel and uploads videos to it (YouTube Data API, youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes).
TikTok
Reads basic profile information to confirm which account is connected (user.info.basic) and posts videos to that account (video.upload, video.publish). Before each post the operator sets title, privacy level and interaction settings as required by TikTok's Content Sharing Guidelines.
Instagram
Reads the connected professional account (instagram_business_basic) and publishes Reels to it (instagram_business_content_publish).

What it stores

OAuth access and refresh tokens for the operator's own accounts, kept on the operator's own computer and used only to make the calls listed above. Clip files and their captions are stored locally until they are published, then deleted. No data is transmitted to any party other than the platform that receives the post.

What it does not do

  • No access to any account other than the operator's.
  • No collection of data about viewers, followers or other users.
  • No use of platform data to train models, build profiles or for any purpose beyond publishing the operator's own clips.
  • No sharing, selling or transfer of data to third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account stays connected and are deleted when access is revoked. The operator can revoke access at any time from YouTube (Google Account → Security → Third-party access), TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or Instagram (Settings → Apps and websites). Revoking access invalidates the stored tokens immediately. To request deletion of any data held by this tool, write to the contact below; requests are handled within 30 days.
