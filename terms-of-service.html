GrowthMindsetClips is a private automation operated by its owner for the channel Growth Mindset Clips. It is not a public product, is not offered to third parties, and has no user accounts other than the operator's own social media accounts.

What the tool does

The tool selects segments from publicly available podcast episodes, edits them into short vertical clips with captions and attribution to the original creator, and publishes those clips to the operator's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, at times the operator sets. Every clip is reviewed by the operator before or shortly after it is scheduled, and the operator can edit, reschedule or delete any clip from each platform's own tools at any time.

Who can use it

Only the operator. The application is not distributed, cannot be installed or authorized by other people, and does not expose any public interface, sign-up or API.

Platform rules

Publishing follows each platform's terms and content guidelines: YouTube Terms of Service and API Services Terms, TikTok Terms of Service and Content Sharing Guidelines, and Meta Platform Terms. Content that a platform's guidelines prohibit is not published.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, for the operator's own use, and may be changed, paused or discontinued at any time.
